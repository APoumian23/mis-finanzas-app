# Mis Finanzas

register: product

## Product purpose

Personal finance app for people in Mexico with several credit cards, loans and people who owe them money. It reads bank statements (PDF), keeps every movement, and answers three questions fast: how much can I still spend this month, what do I have to pay next and when, and when will I be debt free.

## Users

People in Mexico who juggle several cards, loans and money lent to family and friends. They use it mostly on the phone, one-handed, right after paying something or in a spare minute, often in bright daylight or in bed at night; sometimes on a laptop to review the month. Not finance experts: bank jargon ("pago para no generar intereses", CAT, mínimo) needs plain Spanish next to it.

## Tone

Plain, direct Mexican Spanish (tú). Numbers first, words second. Honest about bad news (overdue cards, overspending) without drama or guilt. Motivating where it helps (debt-free date).

## Strategic principles

- The answer must be readable in three seconds: one big number per question.
- Every common action is one tap from home: log an expense, log a card payment, log a loan payment, log that someone paid you, upload a statement.
- Prefer preset amounts (minimum, total, monthly payment) over typing.
- Charts must carry their numbers: totals and labels visible without hover.
- Touch first: 44px minimum targets, thumb-reachable bottom actions, bottom sheets instead of dropdowns.
- Everything works in light (default) and dark.

## Anti-references

- Bank apps full of banners and tiny gray text.
- Fintech dashboards that look impressive but hide the number you need.
- Charts without values; color as the only way to tell things apart.
